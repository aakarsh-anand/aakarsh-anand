### Aakarsh Anand

CS PhD student at UCLA, advised by [Sriram Sankararaman](https://sriramlab.dgsom.ucla.edu/). I build foundation
models and statistical methods for biobank-scale health data, aiming for models that predict well and also teach us
about genetics.

**Selected work**

- **Inertia-1** (NeurIPS 2026, co-first author): open foundation models for wearable motion, pretrained on 18M+ hours
  of accelerometry. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06617) · [Code](https://github.com/yang-ai-lab/Inertia-1)
- **FAME** (Nature Genetics 2025): tests whether a variant's effect depends on the rest of the genome, at biobank
  scale. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02411-y) · [Code](https://github.com/sriramlab/FAME)
- **QuadKAST** (Genome Research 2024, co-first author): scalable, interpretable tests for gene–gene interactions.
  [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.279140.124) · [Code](https://github.com/sriramlab/QuadKAST)
- **ENGINE** (Genome Research 2026): learns which mix of environmental exposures modifies genetic effects on a trait.
  [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.282105.126) · [Code](https://github.com/sriramlab/ENGINE)

Most of my current research code stays private until the papers are out.
